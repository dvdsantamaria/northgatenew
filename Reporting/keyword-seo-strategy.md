## 🔍 Competitor Analysis

### Top Competitors (Sydney)
1. **Porter Davis** - Volume builder, lower price point
2. **Metricon** - Volume builder, marketing-heavy
3. **Mintel Constructions** - Custom builder, similar positioning
4. **Stamford Homes** - Custom/high-end

**SEO Gaps Identified:**
- Most competitors focus on "volume" keywords
- Opportunity in "builder-led" and "design and construct"
- Local SEO underutilized in Newcastle market
- FAQ content limited across all competitors

### Top Competitors (Newcastle)
1. **Bower Projects** - Renovation specialist
2. **Local builders** - Limited digital presence
3. **Sydney builders expanding** - Newcastle as secondary

**SEO Opportunities:**
- Strong local presence possible
- Renovation content underserved
- Port Stephens specific keywords available
