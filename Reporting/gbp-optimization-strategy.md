## ❓ Q&A Section Optimization

### Pre-Populated Questions (Add These)

**Q: Do you build in both Sydney and Newcastle?**
A: Yes! We build custom homes primarily in Sydney 
(Northern Beaches, North Shore, Eastern Suburbs) and 
renovations in Newcastle and Port Stephens. Contact us 
to discuss your specific project location.

**Q: What is builder-led delivery?**
A: Unlike traditional builders where you deal with sales 
teams, your project is led directly by our directors 
(Jordan O'Driscoll) from design through handover. This 
means clearer communication, tighter accountability, 
and no surprises.

**Q: Do you offer interior design services?**
A: Yes—we have in-house interior design included with 
every project. Our design team works alongside the 
build team to ensure your vision is realized without 
having to coordinate multiple contractors.

**Q: What is your typical project budget?**
A: Our custom home projects in Sydney typically range 
from $1.5M to $5M+. Newcastle renovations average 
around $500K. We provide transparent, fixed-price 
contracts with detailed breakdowns.

**Q: How long does a custom home take to build?**
A: Custom homes typically take 12-18 months from 
design approval to handover, depending on complexity 
and site conditions. We provide detailed construction 
schedules at contract signing.

**Q: Do you handle approvals and permits?**
A: Absolutely. We manage all council approvals, 
compliance certifications, and permits as part of 
our comprehensive service.

### Q&A Monitoring
- Check weekly for new questions
- Respond within 24 hours
- Use keywords naturally in answers
- Upvote helpful questions
